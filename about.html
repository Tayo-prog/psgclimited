About P&S Group of Companies

P&S Group of Companies (PSGC) is a developing consumer goods organisation focused on the creation and management of product brands within the hygiene, household care and personal care sectors.

Our Story

Developing brands that support hygiene and care

The company focuses on identifying everyday needs and developing products that provide practical solutions for maintaining clean and comfortable environments.

PSGC collaborates with experienced manufacturing partners to develop products designed for reliability, practicality and everyday use.
