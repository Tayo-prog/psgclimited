Our Story

A more dependable standard of commercial cleaning

WhiteShield Commercial Cleaning was established to provide organisations with a more dependable standard of commercial cleaning.

As a P&S Group company, we combine disciplined planning, professional service and close attention to detail. We understand that the condition of your premises reflects the organisation behind them.

Our objective is simple: to deliver clean, presentable and well-maintained environments that allow our clients to operate with confidence.

Built Around Your Business

A Cleaning Programme Built Around Your Business

Every property has different priorities. Some organisations require daily workplace cleaning, while others need periodic deep cleaning, industrial support or a time-sensitive post-construction service. We begin by understanding your premises, working hours, access arrangements and expectations, then create a clear cleaning plan based on the services and frequency your organisation requires.
